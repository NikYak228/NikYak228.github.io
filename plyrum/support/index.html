Do I need an account?

No. Plyrum has no account and does not create one.

Which AI services work with Plyrum?

Apple's on-device model on supported devices, OpenRouter (sign in or paste a key), and any service with an OpenAI-compatible API: enter its address and your key. Features such as web search, images and files depend on what the service offers.

Where are my chats and keys stored?

On your device only. Keys are kept in the iOS Keychain. Messages go directly from your device to the service you connected.

How do I report a response?

Tap "Report" under the response. It is hidden on your device and you can e-mail us a report.

How do I erase my data?

Delete a chat or a connection, use "Erase all data" in Settings, or delete the app. This does not delete data the service has already received or revoke the key at the service; do that in the service's dashboard.
