Plyrum for iOS and iPadOS — Privacy Policy

Version ios-2026-10-21.1 dated 21 October 2026. Effective on publication.

1. Who we are

The Plyrum app for iPhone and iPad (the "App") is published by Nikita Yakimenko, an individual (solo) developer from the Republic of Kazakhstan ("we"). Privacy contact: Nikitayakimenko2356@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.

This policy covers only the App. It is a stand-alone client for compatible AI services. It has no chat server of its own.

2. In short

  • The App has no account and does not create one.
  • We do not receive your chats, attachments or API keys (service access codes) through the App. History, settings and result files stay on your device.
  • Requests go directly to the service you connect. The App shows its address and asks for explicit consent before sharing data.
  • The Apple local model, speech recognition and read-aloud run on device. Audio is not sent away for recognition.
  • The App has no ads, analytics or tracking. We do not sell data.
  • If you send a support e-mail, we receive its contents and your sender address.

AI service. You choose the compatible API address and key. You may sign in with OpenRouter or enter an address and key manually. The message, current chat history and selected attachments go directly to that service. It also sees connection data, such as your IP address. Its own terms, retention rules and privacy policy apply. It may share data with other companies that help it. Processing may take place outside your country. We cannot guarantee the level of data protection provided by your chosen service. Read its documents before connecting. We do not receive a copy of these requests.

Sign in with OpenRouter. OpenRouter's page opens. Sign-in uses PKCE, a way to protect the exchange of a one-time authorization code for an API key. After you sign in, OpenRouter returns you to the App through an address on the App's website, nikyak228.github.io. The App catches this address itself, so the page usually does not even load. If opened in a browser, it only shows a message: it has no scripts and stores nothing. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's privacy statement applies when it is opened. The App exchanges the code for a key directly with OpenRouter and stores the key in Keychain. The API key never reaches the website. OpenRouter's privacy policy applies to sign-in.

On-device model. On supported devices, you can choose Apple's built-in model. Its processing of messages does not use the network. This does not make connected online services local.

Search, images and response stages. Your chosen service runs web search and image generation when it supports them. It may send needed data to other companies that help it. The service may charge for each operation. Before enabling a tool, the App shows the price available from the service. Source links and step-by-step response stages appear when the service supplies them. A source link you open leads to an external site with its own data practices.

Result files. Images and documents may be downloaded from the service. The App can also create PDF, DOCX, PPTX and XLSX on device and display HTML files without running scripts. Local file creation does not itself send the file online. HTML previews do not run scripts. Files are stored locally. The share sheet sends your selected file to the app or recipient you choose. Save to Photos adds an image to your library with your permission. Later library sync depends on your system settings.

Speech. Voice input uses Apple speech recognition on device only. If local recognition is unavailable, there is no network fallback. You can type instead. Read-aloud also runs on device.

Crash reports and diagnostics. We do not receive crash reports or diagnostics from the App, except anonymous crash reports that Apple may share with developers if you opted in to “Share with App Developers” in iOS settings. This is Apple’s own feature. You can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

4. Consent and withdrawal

Consent is requested for each address before data is sent to the service. A changed address needs new consent. Before consent, no messages, attachments or key are sent to that service. The sheet describes enabled tools and files. Search and image generation start off. A cost notice appears before you enable them.

A service may ask you to confirm an estimated request cost. Cost confirmation does not replace consent to share data. If you decline, nothing is sent and the request is not repeated.

To withdraw consent, delete the connection in Settings. This removes its key and consent record from the device. You can change microphone, speech and Photos add permissions in iOS Settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data is kept until you delete it. You can delete a chat, delete a connection, erase all data in Settings, or delete the App. Use connection deletion or full erasure to remove keys. Deleting the App alone does not guarantee Keychain removal.

Full erasure removes local chats, attachments, result files, settings, keys and consent records. Copies saved to Photos, Files or other apps must be deleted separately. System backups follow your system settings.

Deletion in the App does not revoke a service key or delete data the service already received. Contact that service under its own rules.

Support e-mails and reports are kept for up to 180 days after the last message, then deleted.

6. Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over.

7. Security

Keys are stored in Keychain on this device only and are accessible only while it is unlocked. Service connections use HTTPS only. The App does not log keys or request contents. Do not send keys to support. Check the service address before sending sensitive data.

8. Your rights

Our processing of personal data is subject to the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V of 21 May 2013 "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

You may ask what data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. This is the data in your support e-mails. Contact Nikitayakimenko2356@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days. Contact your chosen service about its data. If your rights are violated, you may complain to the authorized body for personal data protection of the Republic of Kazakhstan (the Ministry of Digital Development, Innovations and Aerospace Industry, its Information Security Committee) or to the data protection authority of your own country.

9. Changes

The new version is posted on this page with a new date. For material changes, the App shows a notice.
